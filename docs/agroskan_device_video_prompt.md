# AgroSkan qurilma demo videosi — 1 daqiqalik, sahna-sahna prompt

Ko'pchilik AI video generatorlar (Sora, Veo, Kling, Runway) bitta so'rovda 5-15
soniyalik klip beradi. 1 daqiqalik video olish uchun quyidagi 6 ta sahnani
**alohida-alohida generatsiya qilib**, keyin video-montaj dasturida (CapCut,
Premiere, DaVinci Resolve) ketma-ket ulang. Har biri ~10 soniya = jami ~60 soniya.

Barcha sahnalarda bir xil uslub saqlanishi uchun har bir promptga quyidagi
"uslub blokini" ildiz sifatida qoldiring.

---

## Uslub bloki (har bir sahna oxiriga qo'shiladi)

```
Style: premium agri-tech product video, photorealistic 4K, soft diffused
greenhouse daylight, shallow depth of field, smooth camera movement, no people,
no text overlays, realistic physics, quiet confident automation, tone similar
to a high-end robotics / Apple hardware commercial.
```

---

## Sahna 1 (0:00–0:10) — Umumiy reja, qurilmani tanishtirish

```
Wide establishing shot inside a modern commercial greenhouse, neat rows of
tomato plants on trellis strings stretching into the distance, gentle humidity
haze in the air. Mounted on an aluminum rail along the ceiling truss, a sleek
white-and-dark-green robotic scanner unit with a glowing emerald LED status
ring sits at the start of the row, then begins gliding slowly forward.

Style: premium agri-tech product video, photorealistic 4K, soft diffused
greenhouse daylight, shallow depth of field, smooth camera movement, no people,
no text overlays, realistic physics, quiet confident automation, tone similar
to a high-end robotics / Apple hardware commercial.
```

## Sahna 2 (0:10–0:20) — Skanerlash ritmi

```
Medium tracking shot following the robotic scanner unit as it glides along the
ceiling rail above a row of tomato plants. It pauses directly above a plant, a
soft white LED flash fires, the gimbal camera head tilts down to frame the
plant, then it smoothly continues to the next position. Repeat this
scan-pause-flash-continue rhythm three times.

Style: premium agri-tech product video, photorealistic 4K, soft diffused
greenhouse daylight, shallow depth of field, smooth camera movement, no people,
no text overlays, realistic physics, quiet confident automation, tone similar
to a high-end robotics / Apple hardware commercial.
```

## Sahna 3 (0:20–0:30) — Kamera yaqinlashuvi (close-up)

```
Extreme close-up shot of the robot's gimbal camera lens, ring light glowing
softly, focusing on a tomato truss with condensation droplets on the leaves and
tomatoes in different ripeness stages (green, orange, red). Rack focus from the
lens to the tomatoes as the shutter appears to click.

Style: premium agri-tech product video, photorealistic 4K, soft diffused
greenhouse daylight, shallow depth of field, smooth camera movement, no people,
no text overlays, realistic physics, quiet confident automation, tone similar
to a high-end robotics / Apple hardware commercial.
```

## Sahna 4 (0:30–0:40) — Pastdan yuqoriga qarab (hero shot)

```
Low-angle tracking shot looking up at the greenhouse ceiling rail system,
showing the robotic scanner's smooth linear motion against structural beams and
grow lights, LED ring glowing against the bright diffused sky light above.

Style: premium agri-tech product video, photorealistic 4K, soft diffused
greenhouse daylight, shallow depth of field, smooth camera movement, no people,
no text overlays, realistic physics, quiet confident automation, tone similar
to a high-end robotics / Apple hardware commercial.
```

## Sahna 5 (0:40–0:50) — Yon tomondan parallaks

```
Side tracking shot at plant height, slow parallax as the camera moves parallel
to the robotic scanner gliding along the rail above, rows of tomato plants
blurring softly in the foreground, the device's emerald LED ring a constant
steady glow as it advances.

Style: premium agri-tech product video, photorealistic 4K, soft diffused
greenhouse daylight, shallow depth of field, smooth camera movement, no people,
no text overlays, realistic physics, quiet confident automation, tone similar
to a high-end robotics / Apple hardware commercial.
```

## Sahna 6 (0:50–1:00) — Yakuniy umumiy reja

```
Wide final establishing shot: the robotic scanner continues down the long
diminishing rows of the greenhouse, LED ring glowing steadily, warm
late-afternoon light streaming through the polycarbonate roof panels, gentle
slow dolly-out revealing the full scale of the automated greenhouse.

Style: premium agri-tech product video, photorealistic 4K, soft diffused
greenhouse daylight, shallow depth of field, smooth camera movement, no people,
no text overlays, realistic physics, quiet confident automation, tone similar
to a high-end robotics / Apple hardware commercial.
```

## Agar vaqt tanqis bo'lsa (tezkor variant)

Faqat Sahna 1 + Sahna 2 + Sahna 6 ni generatsiya qilib, 3 ta klipni ulab,
~30 soniyalik ixcham versiya sifatida ko'rsatish ham yetarli bo'ladi.
