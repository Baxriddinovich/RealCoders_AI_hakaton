# AgroSkan — 30-40 soniyalik variant (3 sahna)

Har birini alohida generatsiya qilib ulang (~10-13 soniyadan).

## Sahna 1 — Tanishtirish + harakat boshlanishi

```
Cinematic 4K photorealistic shot inside a modern commercial greenhouse, neat
rows of tomato plants on trellis strings stretching into the distance, soft
diffused daylight, gentle humidity haze. Mounted on an aluminum ceiling rail,
a sleek white-and-dark-green robotic scanner unit with a glowing emerald LED
ring begins gliding smoothly forward above the row, camera doing a slow dolly
following it.

Style: premium agri-tech product video, shallow depth of field, smooth camera
movement, no people, no text overlays, realistic physics, quiet confident
automation, tone like a high-end robotics / Apple hardware commercial.
```

## Sahna 2 — Skanerlash + yaqin kadr

```
The robotic ceiling-rail scanner pauses above a tomato plant, a soft LED flash
fires, its gimbal camera head tilts down to frame the plant, then glides to the
next position — repeat twice. Cut to an extreme close-up of the camera lens
with condensation droplets on leaves and tomatoes in green, orange and red
ripeness stages, rack focus from lens to fruit.

Style: premium agri-tech product video, photorealistic 4K, shallow depth of
field, smooth camera movement, no people, no text overlays, realistic physics,
quiet confident automation, tone like a high-end robotics / Apple hardware
commercial.
```

## Sahna 3 — Hero shot + yakun

```
Low-angle tracking shot looking up at the greenhouse ceiling rail system, the
robotic scanner's LED ring glowing as it moves smoothly against structural
beams and grow lights, then cut to a wide final shot of it continuing down the
long diminishing rows of the greenhouse in warm late-afternoon light, slow
dolly-out revealing the full scale of the automated greenhouse.

Style: premium agri-tech product video, photorealistic 4K, shallow depth of
field, smooth camera movement, no people, no text overlays, realistic physics,
quiet confident automation, tone like a high-end robotics / Apple hardware
commercial.
```
